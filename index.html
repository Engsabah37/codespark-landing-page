Common questions

Do I need any experience before joining?

No. Web Foundations and QA & Testing assume zero coding background. The React track assumes you've finished Web Foundations or know equivalent basics.

Are the sessions live or recorded?

Live, with a small group and real-time Q&A. Sessions are recorded so you can review them, but the core experience is live.

What if I fall behind?

Instructors hold weekly office hours, and cohorts are small enough that catching up with 1:1 help is realistic.

Is there a certificate at the end?

Yes, every track ends with a certificate of completion once your final project is reviewed and approved.
